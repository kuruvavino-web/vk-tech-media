Legal, Privacy & Payout Policies

Transparent governance policies for advertisers, affiliate creators, and promotional campaign participants.

1. Terms & Conditions

  • All marketing campaigns executed by VK Tech Media Digital Marketing are governed by formal Insertion Orders (IOs) and official attribution reports from partner advertisers.
  • Creator commissions and service fees are payable only upon verified conversion delivery and submission of valid billing details (PAN, Bank Account/UPI, and SAC 998361 invoice).
  • Promotional cashback or user rewards are offered strictly as a marketing incentive upon successful verification of the required campaign milestone (such as completed KYC or account activation).
  • Any fraudulent, self-referred, duplicate, or policy-violating traffic will be disqualified without payout.

2. Privacy Policy

  • Data Collected: We collect essential business and beneficiary information including Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, PAN (for tax compliance), and Bank Account / UPI ID solely for campaign attribution, GST/TDS accounting, and payout processing.
  • Data Protection: We never sell, rent, or share personal or financial details of creators, clients, or campaign users with unauthorized third parties.
  • Secure Processing: All disbursements are routed through RBI-regulated banking and payment gateway partners with complete transaction reference tracking.

3. Payout, Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • Settlement Timeline: Verified influencer settlements and approved user cashback rewards are processed within 2 to 7 business days following conversion confirmation.
  • Failed / Reversed Payouts: If a transfer fails due to an invalid UPI ID or bank detail entered by a beneficiary, the payout is re-initiated once updated details are verified.
  • Advertiser Cancellations & Refunds: Client campaign advances, GST components, and unutilized budgets are reconciled and refunded or adjusted strictly according to the signed campaign agreement.
